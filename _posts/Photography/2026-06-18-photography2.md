These are selected images from my AP Photography Portfolio of 2025-2026. This is a brief description I wrote for the post on Instagram:

"All images shot on a Canon Model 7, processed with CS41, and printed to Adox paper with RA-4. These are scans of the prints.
The goal of this portfolio was to capture the sense of curiosity in isolation--- an anxious step forward we can only take after immense grief. Is there solace in an empty horizon?"

I submitted this portfolio in a really turbulent time in my life where I felt I had lost the few people who gave me purpose. This portfolio is a reflection on finding new paths away from the path we thought to follow.
